ACM ICPC Team — Question

You are given n people and m topics. Each person is represented by a binary string of length m, where:

1 means the person knows the topic.
0 means the person does not know the topic.

A team consists of exactly two people. A team knows a topic if at least one of its members knows it.

Find:

The maximum number of topics any team can know.
The number of teams that know this maximum number of topics.

Input:

4 5
10101
11100
11010
00101

Output:

5
2
